Transparent Capital Layer

“Where is my capital?” should never need a support ticket.

Capital is always in an explicit, named state — allocated by you, per trading mode. Never one ambiguous balance.

Total Capital

Everything recognized for you, across all states.

Trading Allocation

Capital you deliberately assign to a trading mode.

Reserved Margin

Held against your current trading obligations.

Settlement Pending

Withdrawable

Eligible to leave the platform right now.

Unallocated

Allocation is a risk boundary, not a guarantee. In extreme conditions — gaps, slippage, liquidation mechanics — losses can exceed intended limits.

Execution & liquidity

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is ELVA live? Can I deposit?

No. ELVA is in a closed, invite-only beta and runs as a demonstration environment. It does not accept deposits, does not hold client money and does not provide investment services or advice.

Are my trades on the blockchain?

No. Supported capital movements (deposits, withdrawals, settlement) can be on-chain. Trading executes in professional off-chain infrastructure — MT5 at launch. The two layers are reconciled continuously, and we never present one as the other.

Can the AI withdraw or move my funds?

Never. AI and bots have no withdrawal path by architecture — it is not a setting that can be switched on. Withdrawals require your custody controls and security verification.

What happens if the AI goes down?

Manual trading continues unaffected. ELVA Intelligence is a layer on top of the platform, not a dependency of it.

What happens if the Risk Engine goes down?

Automated execution stops — it fails closed. Analysis remains available and manual trading is unaffected.

Does the AI predict profits?

No. ELVA Intelligence explains setups, builds counter-cases and defines invalidation conditions. Any confidence score is model confidence in the setup — not a probability of profit. Trading involves significant risk of loss.

Who can join the beta?

Access is invite-only and subject to eligibility checks, including nationality and residence restrictions. Some jurisdictions cannot be served.
